# AgentTape terms

AgentTape is provided under the MIT License and on a best-effort basis.

AgentTape produces structural evidence from recorded events. It does not provide bit-exact or complete replay, regenerate downstream model reasoning, or capture every hosted tool and external state. Do not use AgentTape output as the sole basis for legal, medical, financial, security, employment, or other consequential decisions.

Users are responsible for reviewing and redacting a `.tape` before sharing it or sending it to a remote endpoint. Do not submit credentials, private production data, or content you are not authorized to process.

The remote MCP may reject malformed, oversized, unsupported, or insufficiently redacted input. Availability and behavior may change between pre-release versions.
